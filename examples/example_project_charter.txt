PROJECT CHARTER

OBJECTIVES
- Automate invoice processing for AP team (OCR + validation + workflow approvals).
- Reduce manual data entry errors and rework.
- Improve turnaround time for invoice approvals.
- Provide audit trail for approvals and segregation of duties.

IN SCOPE
- Ingest invoices via email and portal upload.
- Extract key fields and validate against PO and vendor master data.
- Approval workflow with SLA and escalation.
- Reporting dashboard for cycle time and exception rates.

OUT OF SCOPE
- Replacing the ERP.
- Changing vendor onboarding process.

NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
- Availability 99.9%
- Access controls (role-based), audit logs retained for 7 years.
